# Topic: Innovation in consulting for insurance clients

**Possible Title:** *How consulting firms can better lead innovation in insurance clients*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- What consulting practices improve innovation outcomes for insurers.
- How consultants build trust and momentum for change.
- What makes consulting-led innovation sustainable after go-live.

## Stronger framing
This topic looks at consulting as a delivery mechanism for innovation. It is especially strong for someone working at CGI because it connects your professional experience with your academic work.

## Possible problem statement
Consulting projects may deliver recommendations or technology, but the client may struggle to embed the change. The problem is understanding what consulting firms must do to create lasting innovation value.

## Possible research questions
- What consulting behaviors best support innovation adoption?
- How do trust and communication affect client outcomes?
- How can consulting firms improve sustainability of change?
- What role does governance play in consulting-led innovation?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Client-consultant trust.
- Knowledge transfer and capability building.
- Engagement governance.
- Value realization after implementation.

## Why this is strong
It is highly relevant to your current role and offers a useful practitioner contribution. It also stands out because it focuses on the consulting side of innovation, not just the client side.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Access to client and consulting perspectives can be difficult. There is also a risk of making the topic too close to a project review rather than a dissertation.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How consulting firms enable sustainable innovation in insurance clients.
- The role of trust and governance in consulting-led transformation.
- What makes innovation stick after a consulting engagement ends.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To understand how consulting firms can better lead and sustain innovation in insurance client organizations.

## Likely contribution
Insights into consulting practices that improve adoption, capability transfer, and long-term value.

## Best methodology options
- Case study within one consulting engagement or client program.
- Interviews with consultants, client stakeholders, and sponsors.
- Review of project artifacts and post-implementation outcomes.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: consulting, client adoption, trust, governance, transformation sustainability, value realization.
