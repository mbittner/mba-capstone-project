# Topic: Resistance to change in regulated industries

**Possible Title:** *Understanding resistance to innovation in insurance and how leaders can reduce it*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- Why employees resist innovation in insurance environments.
- How regulation and risk culture increase resistance.
- Leadership actions that reduce resistance and improve adoption.

## Stronger framing
This topic focuses on resistance as a predictable response to change rather than a simple attitude problem. Insurance is a strong setting because caution, compliance, and control are part of the operating model.

## Possible problem statement
Resistance can slow or derail innovation initiatives even when the business case is strong. Leaders need to understand the causes of resistance and how to respond to them effectively.

## Possible research questions
- What are the main causes of resistance to innovation?
- How do regulation and risk concerns shape resistance?
- Which leadership interventions reduce resistance most effectively?
- How does communication influence employee acceptance?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Employee psychology and change readiness.
- Risk aversion and compliance culture.
- Communication and engagement approaches.
- Manager role in overcoming resistance.

## Why this is strong
It is practical and easy to link to real transformation programs. It also creates room for useful recommendations on communication and leadership.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Resistance can be interpreted too broadly unless it is tied to one change context. There is also a risk of focusing only on symptoms rather than root causes.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Leadership strategies for reducing resistance to innovation in insurance.
- How regulatory culture shapes resistance to change.
- Employee resistance and adoption in insurance transformation programs.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To explore why resistance to innovation occurs in insurance and what leaders can do to reduce it.

## Likely contribution
A practical map of resistance drivers and leadership responses in regulated industries.

## Best methodology options
- Semi-structured interviews with change participants.
- Case study of a resisted or partially adopted initiative.
- Thematic analysis around barriers, emotions, and leadership responses.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: resistance to change, risk culture, compliance, adoption, employee engagement, communication.
