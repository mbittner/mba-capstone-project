# Dissertation Proposal: Innovation from Within

## Title
**When to Lead Internal Innovation vs. Acquire External Solutions in Insurance**

---

## 1. Research Background and Context

### 1.1 The Problem in Insurance
Insurance leaders face recurring build-versus-buy decisions: Should we develop data analytics capability in-house or partner with a vendor? Should we build our own cloud platform or adopt a SaaS solution? Should we innovate internally or acquire technology?

These decisions are complex: speed to market favors external acquisition; control and strategic fit favor internal development; cost and capability vary case by case. Yet many organizations lack a clear strategic framework for deciding—they make choices inconsistently, waste resources on wrong bets, and miss opportunities.

This dissertation examines how insurance leaders decide between internal innovation and external solutions and what frameworks support better strategic choices.

### 1.2 Why This Topic Is Important
1. **Strategic Thinking Theme**: Build-versus-buy is a fundamental strategic decision with major business impact.
2. **Repeated Problem**: Insurance leaders face this decision constantly; a framework would create immediate value.
3. **Complex Decision**: No single right answer; context matters enormously.
4. **Consulting Application**: Consulting firms advise on these choices; understanding the framework improves advisory.
5. **Resource Allocation**: Gets at how organizations prioritize and invest strategic resources.

---

## 2. Research Question and Objectives

### 2.1 Primary Research Question
**What criteria and decision-making frameworks do insurance leaders use to determine whether to develop innovation internally or acquire/partner for external solutions, and which frameworks most consistently produce strategically sound decisions?**

### 2.2 Research Objectives

**Objective 1 (Literature):** To critically review literature on build-versus-buy decisions, strategic make-or-buy choices, organizational capability assessment, and vendor management in regulated and technology-intensive industries.

**Objective 2 (Evidence):** To explore how insurance leaders actually approach and decide on internal versus external innovation, including decision criteria, stakeholder involvement, and post-decision outcomes.

**Objective 3 (Analysis):** To identify which decision criteria and frameworks correlate with successful outcomes and what factors influence choices.

**Objective 4 (Contribution):** To develop a practical decision framework for internal versus external innovation in insurance.

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Research Design
**Comparative case study of build-versus-buy decisions**: Study 3-4 decisions (some internal, some external, some mixed) in insurance to understand what criteria drove choices and how outcomes differed.

### 4.2 Data Collection

#### Primary Data: Interviews
- **Sample**: 16-20 interviews across multiple decisions including:
  - Decision-makers (strategy, business, technical leaders)
  - For internal innovations: development team leads
  - For external: vendor managers, implementation leads
  - Business owners who use the resulting solutions

- **Interview Guide Topics**:
  - How the internal-vs-external decision was framed
  - What criteria influenced the choice
  - Why that route was chosen (speed, control, cost, etc.)
  - Regrets or satisfaction post-decision
  - Outcomes (time to value, control, cost, quality)
  - What would have changed the decision

#### Secondary Data: Decision Documents
- Business cases for decisions
- Capability assessments (if conducted)
- Vendor evaluation criteria
- Risk assessments
- Post-implementation reviews

#### Tertiary Data: Outcome Data
- Time to value (internal vs. external solutions)
- Cost outcomes (budget vs. actual)
- Quality and capability outcomes
- Control and flexibility outcomes

### 4.3 Analysis Approach
- **Decision Criteria Extraction**: What factors actually drove choices.
- **Outcome Mapping**: Link decision factors to outcomes.
- **Comparative Analysis**: Identify patterns across decisions.
- **Framework Development**: Extract a decision model from the cases.

## 5. Ethical Considerations

**Key safeguards**:
