# Topic: Strategic thinking in uncertain environments

**Possible Title:** *Strategic thinking for innovation under uncertainty in the insurance industry*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How leaders make innovation decisions in uncertain conditions.
- How insurers balance short-term stability with long-term change.
- How strategic thinking supports innovation in regulated markets.

## Stronger framing
This topic positions strategic thinking as the capability that helps leaders make good choices when the future is unclear. Insurance is a strong context because uncertainty, regulation, and risk management are built into the industry.

## Possible problem statement
Leaders often face competing priorities, incomplete information, and multiple innovation options. Without a strong strategic lens, organizations may invest in the wrong initiatives or move too slowly.

## Possible research questions
- How do leaders use strategic thinking when deciding on innovation priorities?
- What frameworks help manage uncertainty in insurance transformation?
- How do leaders balance exploration and operational stability?
- What role does scenario planning play in decision-making?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Scenario planning and risk assessment.
- Portfolio prioritization.
- Short-term versus long-term trade-offs.
- Decision-making under incomplete information.

## Why this is strong
It has a strong MBA fit because it connects strategy, leadership, and innovation. It also speaks directly to the realities of insurance management.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
The concept of strategic thinking can be broad and hard to operationalize. It needs a tight context, such as a specific innovation program or decision-making process.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Strategic decision-making for innovation under uncertainty in insurance.
- How leaders prioritize innovation when business conditions are unclear.
- Scenario-based strategic thinking in insurance transformation.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To explore how strategic thinking helps insurance leaders make better innovation decisions under uncertainty.

## Likely contribution
A clearer understanding of how strategy tools can support innovation choices in a regulated industry.

## Best methodology options
- Qualitative interviews with senior leaders and transformation managers.
- Case study of a strategic initiative or portfolio review process.
- Document analysis of strategy decks, business cases, and governance materials.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: strategic thinking, uncertainty, scenario planning, portfolio management, risk, decision-making.
