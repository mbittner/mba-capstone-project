# Topic: Building organizational agility in insurance

**Possible Title:** *Leadership strategies for building organizational agility in legacy insurance systems*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How agile behaviors can be adapted for regulated environments.
- How leaders remove structural barriers to agility.
- How insurance firms stay responsive without losing control.

## Stronger framing
This topic explores agility as an organizational capability rather than just a delivery method. It suits insurance because the industry must respond quickly while keeping risk, compliance, and service quality under control.

## Possible problem statement
Legacy structures can slow decision-making and reduce responsiveness. Leaders need to understand which organizational conditions make agility possible in insurance.

## Possible research questions
- What does organizational agility mean in insurance contexts?
- Which leadership actions improve responsiveness?
- How do legacy systems and compliance requirements constrain agility?
- What structural changes support faster decision-making?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Decision speed and empowerment.
- Cross-functional collaboration.
- Governance and control.
- Agile practices in regulated settings.

## Why this is strong
It is current, practical, and attractive to both innovation and leadership readers. It also links well to transformation challenges in mature firms.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Agility can mean many things, so the scope must be defined carefully. The topic may also drift into pure delivery methodology if not tied back to leadership and organization design.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Leadership practices that improve organizational agility in insurance.
- How regulated firms can become more agile without weakening control.
- Removing structural barriers to agility in legacy insurers.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To explore how leaders can build organizational agility in insurance while preserving governance and control.

## Likely contribution
A practical view of agility in regulated, legacy-heavy organizations.

## Best methodology options
- Case study of an agility or transformation initiative.
- Interviews with leaders, product owners, and delivery teams.
- Analysis of operating model, governance, and process changes.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: agility, responsiveness, operating model, governance, legacy systems, adaptive leadership.
