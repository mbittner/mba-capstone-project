# Topic: Diversity and inclusion as innovation drivers

**Possible Title:** *The relationship between diverse leadership and innovation outcomes in insurance consulting*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How diversity of perspective improves innovation quality.
- How inclusive leadership affects collaboration and ideas.
- How diverse teams contribute to better innovation outcomes.

## Stronger framing
This topic connects leadership and culture to innovation performance. It is relevant because diverse and inclusive teams often bring broader perspectives, but only if the environment allows those perspectives to be used.

## Possible problem statement
Organizations may improve diversity representation without seeing innovation benefits if inclusion is weak. The challenge is understanding how diversity translates into better ideas, decisions, and outcomes.

## Possible research questions
- How does leadership diversity influence innovation outcomes?
- What role does inclusion play in turning diversity into value?
- Which barriers stop diverse perspectives from shaping innovation?
- How can organizations measure the relationship between inclusion and innovation?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Inclusive leadership behaviors.
- Team composition and idea generation.
- Barriers to participation.
- Link between inclusion and performance.

## Why this is strong
It is contemporary, important, and connects directly to culture and leadership. It also has clear practical implications for team design and leadership behavior.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Diversity and innovation data can be sensitive, and causality is hard to prove. The topic also needs care to avoid becoming a broad HR discussion.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How inclusive leadership supports innovation in insurance consulting.
- The impact of diverse leadership teams on innovation outcomes.
- Turning diversity into innovation through inclusive culture.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To examine how diversity and inclusion contribute to innovation outcomes in insurance consulting.

## Likely contribution
A practical link between inclusive leadership, team dynamics, and innovation performance.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with leaders and team members.
- Case study of diverse or inclusive teams involved in innovation.
- Optional survey if access to broader employee data is available.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: inclusion, diversity, innovation performance, team dynamics, inclusive leadership.
