## 1. Leading digital change in insurance

**Possible Title:** *How leadership influences successful digital transformation in insurance consulting*

**Focus:**
- What leadership behaviors help or hinder transformation?
- How consulting leaders drive adoption of new technology in insurers
- Barriers and enablers specific to the insurance industry

**Why It Works:**
- Strong fit with innovation + change + leadership MBA themes
- Directly useful in your role as a director at CGI
- Highly relevant to insurance clients' current priorities
