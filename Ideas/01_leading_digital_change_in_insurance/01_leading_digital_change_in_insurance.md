# Topic: Leading digital change in insurance

**Possible Title:** *How leadership influences successful digital transformation in insurance consulting*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- Leadership behaviors that increase adoption during digital transformation.
- How consulting leaders guide insurers through technology-enabled change.
- What differentiates successful from stalled digital programs in insurance.

## Stronger framing
This topic frames digital transformation as a leadership and change problem rather than only a technology problem. It is especially relevant in insurance where legacy systems, regulation, and risk appetite shape adoption.

## Possible problem statement
Insurance organizations often invest in digital programs but struggle to convert them into sustained operating change. Weak sponsorship, unclear priorities, and inconsistent engagement can undermine adoption and business value.

## Possible research questions
- Which leadership behaviors support successful digital transformation?
- How do leaders influence employee and stakeholder adoption of new technology?
- What barriers are specific to insurance digital change?
- How do consulting-led programs differ from internally led transformations?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Leadership sponsorship and governance.
- Employee adoption and resistance.
- Technology rollout and value realization.
- Insurance-specific constraints such as regulation and legacy platforms.

## Why this is strong
It is current, practical, and tightly aligned to innovation, change, and leadership MBA themes. It also connects directly to your work in consulting and insurance.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
The topic can become too broad if digital transformation is not narrowed to a specific program, function, or business outcome. It may also be difficult to separate leadership effects from technology quality or vendor performance.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Leadership practices that accelerate digital adoption in insurance transformation programs.
- How consulting leaders help insurers convert digital investment into operational change.
- The role of leadership in reducing resistance to digital transformation.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To examine how leadership influences the success of digital transformation in insurance and to identify practices that improve adoption and value realization.

## Likely contribution
A practical leadership framework for digital change in regulated, legacy-heavy environments.

## Best methodology options
- Qualitative interviews with leaders, managers, and delivery stakeholders.
- Single-case or multiple-case study of a digital transformation program.
- Document review of transformation plans, adoption metrics, and governance artifacts.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: digital transformation, adoption, sponsorship, change leadership, insurance modernization, consulting delivery.
