# Topic: Innovation from within: internal vs. external solutions

**Possible Title:** *When to lead internal innovation vs. acquire external solutions in insurance*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How leaders decide whether to build, buy, or partner.
- Which criteria shape internal versus external innovation choices.
- How strategic fit and capability influence sourcing decisions.

## Stronger framing
This topic looks at innovation as a sourcing and capability decision. It is relevant in insurance because firms often balance in-house development, vendor solutions, and strategic partnerships.

## Possible problem statement
Organizations can waste time and money by trying to build everything internally or by outsourcing too much too soon. Leaders need a structured way to decide the best route for each innovation need.

## Possible research questions
- What criteria do leaders use to decide build versus buy versus partner?
- How do risk, capability, and speed affect sourcing choices?
- When is internal innovation preferable in insurance?
- When does external capability create more value?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Strategic fit and capability assessment.
- Speed to value versus long-term control.
- Vendor and partner management.
- Risk, cost, and scalability considerations.

## Why this is strong
It is a strong strategic thinking topic with immediate business relevance. It also creates space for decision frameworks that are useful in consulting conversations.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Sourcing and build-versus-buy decisions are often confidential and highly context-dependent. The topic can become too operational unless you keep the strategic framing clear.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Decision frameworks for build versus buy innovation choices in insurance.
- How insurance leaders choose between internal development and external solutions.
- Strategic criteria for sourcing innovation capability.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To understand how insurance leaders decide when to innovate internally and when to use external solutions.

## Likely contribution
A strategic decision model for balancing speed, capability, cost, and control.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with strategy, architecture, and transformation leaders.
- Case study of one or more sourcing decisions.
- Comparison of successful and less successful build/buy choices.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: build versus buy, partnering, sourcing strategy, capability assessment, vendor management.
