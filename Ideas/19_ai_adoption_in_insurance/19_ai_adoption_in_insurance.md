# Topic: AI Adoption in Insurance

**Possible Title:** *How leadership, trust, and governance shape successful AI adoption in insurance organizations*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How leaders drive adoption of AI tools across teams.
- What makes employees trust and use AI-enabled systems.
- Why AI pilots fail to scale into everyday business practice.

## Stronger framing
This topic focuses on adoption rather than AI technology itself. It examines the leadership, cultural, and organizational conditions that determine whether AI becomes embedded in routine work.

## Possible problem statement
Many insurance firms are investing in AI, but adoption is uneven. Some initiatives remain stuck in pilot mode because employees distrust the tools, leaders lack a clear adoption strategy, or governance processes slow implementation.

## Possible research questions
- What factors most influence AI adoption in insurance organizations?
- How do leadership and communication shape acceptance of AI?
- What barriers prevent AI initiatives from scaling?
- How do trust and governance affect adoption?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Leadership and change management
- Employee trust and readiness
- Governance and risk appetite
- Scaling from pilot to enterprise

## Why this is strong
It is current, practical, and highly relevant to insurance consulting. It also gives you a way to discuss innovation, trust, and change without being tied to one specific AI tool.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
AI is broad and fast-moving, so the scope must be limited to a specific use case, function, or adoption context. Otherwise the topic can become too generic.

## Sharper version of the topic
- What enables successful AI adoption in insurance organizations?
- Leadership strategies for improving AI adoption in regulated industries.
- Why AI pilots fail to scale in insurance.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To explore how insurance organizations can improve AI adoption by aligning leadership, culture, and governance.

## Likely contribution
A practical framework for AI adoption that balances innovation, trust, and organizational readiness.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with business, technology, and change leaders.
- Case study of an AI rollout or adoption programme.
- Document analysis of adoption plans, governance materials, and training approaches.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: AI adoption, generative AI, change management, trust, governance, readiness, scaling, insurance.
