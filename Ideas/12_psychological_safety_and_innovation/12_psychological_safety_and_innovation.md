# Topic: Psychological safety and innovation

**Possible Title:** *The impact of psychological safety on employee-driven innovation in insurance organizations*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How psychological safety affects speaking up and experimentation.
- How leaders create environments where people share ideas.
- How safety relates to innovation adoption and learning.

## Stronger framing
This topic focuses on the human conditions that allow innovation to emerge. Psychological safety is especially useful in insurance where people may avoid risk, mistakes, or visible disagreement.

## Possible problem statement
Employees may have good ideas but remain silent if the environment feels unsafe. Without psychological safety, innovation can be limited to formal initiatives rather than everyday improvement.

## Possible research questions
- How does psychological safety influence innovation behavior?
- Which leadership behaviors increase psychological safety?
- How does safety affect experimentation and learning?
- What role does team climate play in innovation outcomes?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Speaking up and idea sharing.
- Leader inclusiveness.
- Team climate and trust.
- Experimentation and learning from failure.

## Why this is strong
It is a well-supported research area and has direct leadership relevance. It also translates easily into practical recommendations for managers.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Psychological safety is often measured through surveys, and causality can be difficult to prove. The topic needs a clear link to innovation behavior rather than general engagement.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How psychological safety influences employee innovation in insurance.
- Leadership behaviors that build psychological safety for innovation.
- The link between team climate and innovation outcomes.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To examine how psychological safety affects employee-driven innovation and what leaders can do to strengthen it.

## Likely contribution
A clearer view of the leadership conditions that support everyday innovation behavior.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews or focus groups with employees and managers.
- Survey-based study if access is available.
- Mixed-method design to combine climate data with qualitative insight.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: psychological safety, speaking up, team climate, experimentation, trust, inclusive leadership.
