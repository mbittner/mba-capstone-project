# Topic: Innovation portfolio management

**Possible Title:** *Strategic governance of innovation portfolios in insurance firms*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How leaders balance incremental and transformational innovation.
- How portfolio decisions shape innovation success.
- What governance helps prioritize the right initiatives.

## Stronger framing
This topic treats innovation as a portfolio problem rather than a single project problem. That makes it a strong strategic thinking topic because leaders must choose where to invest limited resources.

## Possible problem statement
Insurance firms may run too many pilots, or the wrong mix of initiatives, without a clear portfolio logic. Poor prioritization can waste resources and dilute impact.

## Possible research questions
- How do leaders prioritize innovation initiatives?
- What portfolio mix is most effective in insurance?
- How do governance processes shape innovation choices?
- How are risk, value, and feasibility balanced?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Portfolio governance.
- Incremental versus disruptive innovation.
- Resource allocation and funding decisions.
- Decision criteria and stage gates.

## Why this is strong
It is strategic, practical, and directly useful to leaders making investment decisions. It also provides a more executive-level lens than many change topics.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Access to portfolio data can be sensitive. The study can also become too abstract unless linked to a specific governance mechanism or decision process.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How insurance leaders govern innovation portfolios.
- Balancing incremental and transformational innovation in insurers.
- Decision frameworks for innovation prioritization.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To understand how strategic governance of innovation portfolios improves innovation outcomes in insurance.

## Likely contribution
A decision-oriented framework for selecting and balancing innovation initiatives.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with portfolio owners and executives.
- Case study of an innovation governance process.
- Document review of funding, prioritization, and stage-gate materials.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: portfolio management, governance, prioritization, stage gates, innovation funding, strategic allocation.
