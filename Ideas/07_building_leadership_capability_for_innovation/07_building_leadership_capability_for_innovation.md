# Topic: Building leadership capability for innovation

**Possible Title:** *Developing leaders who can drive innovation in traditional industries*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- What capabilities leaders need to support innovation.
- How organizations can build those capabilities.
- Which development methods actually change leader behavior.

## Stronger framing
This topic shifts the focus from one transformation to the longer-term question of leadership capability. It fits well with succession, leadership development, and organizational learning.

## Possible problem statement
Many organizations expect leaders to drive innovation without giving them the skills or support to do so. As a result, innovation capability stays uneven and dependent on a few individuals.

## Possible research questions
- What capabilities define an innovation-ready leader?
- How can organizations develop those capabilities?
- Which development approaches are most effective?
- How does leadership capability affect innovation outcomes?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Coaching, training, and experiential learning.
- Leader mindset and behaviors.
- Cross-functional collaboration.
- Talent and succession planning.

## Why this is strong
It bridges essence of leadership with practical innovation needs. It also has long-term value because it focuses on leadership development rather than a single project.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Leadership capability is difficult to measure directly. The topic may also become too general unless you define the specific capabilities and industry context clearly.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Leadership capabilities required to sustain innovation in insurance.
- How organizations develop leaders for transformation and innovation.
- Building innovation-ready leadership in traditional industries.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To identify the leadership capabilities needed to drive innovation and how organizations can develop them.

## Likely contribution
A capability framework that supports leadership development and innovation execution.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with leaders, HR, and development professionals.
- Review of leadership competency frameworks and development programs.
- Case study of a leadership development initiative linked to innovation.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: leadership development, capability building, innovation mindset, coaching, succession, talent.
