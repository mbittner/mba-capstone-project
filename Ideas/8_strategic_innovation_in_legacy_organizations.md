# Topic: Strategic innovation in legacy organizations

**Possible Title:** *How legacy insurance firms can balance operational stability with innovation*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How mature insurers balance stability and innovation.
- How leaders decide where to invest in change.
- How strategy and governance shape innovation portfolios.

## Stronger framing
This topic is about tension management: keeping the business running while modernizing it. That makes it highly relevant to legacy insurers with complex operating models and high service expectations.

## Possible problem statement
Legacy organizations often struggle to innovate because resources are tied up in operations and legacy technology. Without strong strategic choices, innovation remains small-scale or fragmented.

## Possible research questions
- How do legacy firms prioritize innovation against operational demands?
- What governance models support strategic innovation?
- How do leaders allocate resources across core and future business needs?
- What trade-offs matter most in insurance modernization?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Strategic prioritization.
- Resource allocation.
- Governance and portfolio oversight.
- Balancing core versus transformational investment.

## Why this is strong
It is highly realistic and strategically rich. It also links well to your experience of working with mature organizations that must evolve without disruption.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
The scope can expand quickly into general strategy unless you focus on one business area or investment decision. Access to strategic information may also be limited.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How legacy insurance organizations prioritize strategic innovation.
- Balancing operational stability and innovation in mature insurers.
- Governance choices that enable innovation in legacy firms.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To understand how legacy insurance organizations can balance operational stability with strategic innovation.

## Likely contribution
A practical lens on how mature firms can make better innovation choices without undermining core operations.

## Best methodology options
- Strategic case study of one legacy organization.
- Interviews with senior leaders and portfolio owners.
- Review of strategic plans, investment cases, and governance documents.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: legacy systems, strategic prioritization, operational stability, governance, portfolio management.
