# Dissertation Proposal: Building Leadership Capability for Innovation

## Title
**Developing Leaders Who Can Drive Innovation in Traditional Industries**

---

## 1. Research Background and Context

### 1.1 The Problem in Insurance
Insurance organizations expect leaders to drive innovation without giving them the skills, training, or support to do so. Innovation leadership requires different capabilities than operational leadership: comfort with ambiguity, experimentation, cross-functional collaboration, and risk tolerance that conflict with traditional insurance discipline.

The result: Innovation capability remains unevenly distributed and dependent on individual leaders' natural ability. Organizations cannot scale innovation because they haven't scaled the leadership capability needed to support it.

This dissertation examines what leadership capabilities support innovation, how they can be developed, and which development methods actually change leader behavior.

### 1.2 Why This Topic Is Important
1. **Strategic Lever**: Leadership capability is foundational; without it, innovation initiatives struggle.
2. **Long-Term Orientation**: Focuses on capability building rather than single projects.
3. **Succession Planning**: Directly relevant to talent development and organizational resilience.
4. **Consulting Application**: Understanding leader development helps consultants advise on capability building.
5. **MBA Connection**: Bridges leadership essence with innovation and organizational strategy.

---

## 2. Research Question and Objectives

### 2.1 Primary Research Question
**What capabilities do leaders need to effectively drive innovation in traditional industries like insurance, and what development approaches most reliably build those capabilities?**

### 2.2 Research Objectives

**Objective 1 (Literature):** To critically review literature on leadership competencies, innovation-specific leadership capabilities, leader development methods (coaching, training, experiential learning), and capability assessment in organizational settings.

**Objective 2 (Evidence):** To identify the specific capabilities innovation-ready leaders demonstrate, and explore which development methods have actually changed leader behavior in insurance or similar organizations.

**Objective 3 (Analysis):** To assess which development approaches are most effective and how to measure progress in capability building.

**Objective 4 (Contribution):** To develop a leadership capability framework for innovation and actionable recommendations for leader development in insurance.

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Research Design
**Case study of a leadership development initiative**: Study how an organization (or multiple organizations) developed leaders for innovation, tracking which methods worked and what barriers existed.

Alternatively, **phenomenological interviews**: Explore how innovation-ready leaders acquired their capabilities and what development helped.

### 4.2 Data Collection

#### Primary Data: Interviews
- **Sample**: 15-20 interviews including:
  - Leaders who participated in development programs
  - HR/talent development leaders running the programs
  - Coaches or trainers
  - Line managers observing leader behavior change
  - Innovation sponsors who work with developed leaders

- **Interview Guide Topics**:
  - What capabilities were targeted in development
  - Which development methods were used
  - What worked to change behavior
  - What didn't work
  - How capability was assessed
  - Real-world impact on innovation outcomes

#### Secondary Data: Development Program Materials
- Curriculum, modules, or coaching frameworks
- Participant feedback and evaluations
- Competency assessments (before/after if available)
- Behavioral change observations from managers

#### Tertiary Data: Organizational Artifacts
- Leadership competency models
- Innovation strategy and leader roles
- Succession plans

### 4.3 Analysis Approach
- **Capability Extraction**: From interviews, identify what capabilities innovation-ready leaders need.
- **Method Effectiveness**: Assess which development methods correlate with capability building.
- **Barrier Analysis**: Identify obstacles to leader development in traditional organizations.
