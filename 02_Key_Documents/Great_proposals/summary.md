# Great Proposals — Summary Table

## Scope

This file summarises the three example MBA/MA dissertation proposals stored in this folder. Each row links to the full summary file. Use the tables to compare the research problems, questions, designs, and frameworks, and to choose the best model for your own proposal.

## Proposals at a glance

| # | Author (Year) | Title | Topic area | Best for |
|---|---------------|-------|------------|----------|
| 1 | [Ahlers (2018)](Ahlers%202018%20_summary.md) | HRD strategies for managerial adaptability | HRD, adaptability, management development, change | Linking a multidimensional adaptability model to a practitioner interview study |
| 2 | [Marshall (2020)](Marshall%202020_summary.md) | Collaborative value creation in Canadian SME–non-profit partnerships | CSR, cross-sector partnerships, shared value, impact measurement | Moving from literature to a practical maturity framework |
| 3 | [Oloyede (2020)](Oloyede%202020_summary.md) | Senior leadership team effectiveness in Nigerian NGOs | Senior leadership teams, NGO management, team effectiveness | A pragmatic, concurrent mixed-method design |

## Research question and objectives

| Author (Year) | Research question | Core objectives |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| [Ahlers (2018)](Ahlers%202018%20_summary.md) | What HRD strategies enable and foster the adaptability of managers within fast-changing for-profit organisations in the Netherlands? | Analyse adaptability literature; evaluate HRD strategies; recommend strategies to foster adaptability |
| [Marshall (2020)](Marshall%202020_summary.md) | How can a Canadian NPO in the Global South and a Canadian SME collaborate to create measurable shared value? | Evaluate collaborative value-creation literature; assess how partnerships can be optimised; develop a maturity framework |
| [Oloyede (2020)](Oloyede%202020_summary.md) | What factors facilitate or inhibit senior leadership team effectiveness in NGOs in Nigeria? | Review team-effectiveness literature; profile the teams studied; recommend improvements |

## Methodology and context

| Author (Year) | Research design | Philosophy and approach | Sample and data | Analysis | Context / sector | Country |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------------------|-----------------|----------|------------------|---------|
| [Ahlers (2018)](Ahlers%202018%20_summary.md) | Exploratory, cross-organisational qualitative study | Interpretivism, inductive | 12 HRD professionals; semi-structured interviews | Thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke) | For-profit organisations, HRD practitioners | Netherlands |
| [Marshall (2020)](Marshall%202020_summary.md) | Cross-sectional, exploratory qualitative study | Interpretivism, inductive | 10 SME CEOs; semi-structured interviews plus observations | Thematic content analysis, literature triangulation | SME–NPO CSR partnerships | Canada (Global South focus) |
| [Oloyede (2020)](Oloyede%202020_summary.md) | Concurrent mixed-method case study | Pragmatism | 15 NGO CEO interviews plus a senior-team survey (≥50% per team) | Coding/thematic analysis plus survey comparison | Start-up/expansion-stage charitable NGOs | Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja) |

## Conceptual framework and key literature

| Author (Year) | Main frameworks and concepts |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| [Ahlers (2018)](Ahlers%202018%20_summary.md) | Van Dam's multidimensional adaptability (cognitive, affective, behavioural); adaptive leadership; personal resources (self-efficacy, resilience, EI); HRD aligned to strategy |
| [Marshall (2020)](Marshall%202020_summary.md) | Carroll's CSR; Porter and Kramer's Creating Shared Value; Visser's Integrated Value Creation; Austin and Seitanidi's Collaborative Value Creation; impact measurement (Balanced Scorecard, theory of change) |
| [Oloyede (2020)](Oloyede%202020_summary.md) | Team-effectiveness models: Wageman, Hackman, Katzenbach and Smith, Mathieu; team leadership; NGO leadership context |

## Strengths and points to watch

| Author (Year) | Key strengths | Key limitations to watch |
|---------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| [Ahlers (2018)](Ahlers%202018%20_summary.md) | Well-aligned question, sample, and method; multi-organisation breadth; acknowledged reflexivity | Single (HRD) perspective; perceived not demonstrated effectiveness; "fast-changing" undefined; no statistical generalisation |
| [Marshall (2020)](Marshall%202020_summary.md) | Clear practical problem; strong theoretical synthesis; tangible framework output; measurement integral | One-sided sample (SME CEOs only); small purposive sample; risk of pre-embedded conclusion; needs NPO validation |
| [Oloyede (2020)](Oloyede%202020_summary.md) | Mixed methods compare leader and team views; concurrent design; life-cycle focus | Small purposive sample; church-network access bias; CEO self-presentation; integration and quantitative plan underspecified |
