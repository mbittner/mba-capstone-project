# Dissertation Proposal: Leadership During Technology Disruption

## Title
**How Insurance Leaders Navigate Digital Transformation and Technology Disruption**

---

## 1. Research Background and Context

### 1.1 The Problem in Insurance
Technology disruption is transforming insurance: cloud computing, artificial intelligence, data analytics, and new business models (InsurTech startups) challenge traditional delivery and operations. Insurance leaders face unprecedented challenges: legacy systems, regulatory constraints, skilled workforce trained on old ways, and boards expecting both transformation and stability.

How do leaders in traditional insurance organizations navigate this disruption? What capabilities, strategies, and mindsets enable them to lead transformation while managing risk and preserving business continuity?

This dissertation examines what leadership practices help insurance organizations survive and thrive during digital disruption.

### 1.2 Why This Topic Is Important
1. **Critical Business Context**: Insurance is under genuine disruption; survival depends on navigating it well.
2. **Leadership Practice**: Disruption requires distinctive leadership capabilities and mindsets.
3. **MBA Themes**: Leadership, strategic thinking, innovation, and change in a high-stakes context.
4. **Generalizable**: Insights apply beyond insurance to other regulated, traditional industries.
5. **Measurable Impact**: Outcomes visible in transformation success, retention of talent and customers.

---

## 2. Research Question and Objectives

### 2.1 Primary Research Question
**What leadership capabilities, strategic choices, and organizational practices enable insurance leaders to successfully navigate digital transformation and technology disruption while managing operational continuity and stakeholder expectations?**

### 2.2 Research Objectives

**Objective 1 (Literature):** To critically review literature on disruptive innovation, digital transformation, leadership in turbulent contexts, organizational ambidexterity, and strategic adaptation in regulated industries.

**Objective 2 (Evidence):** To explore what insurance leaders are actually doing to navigate disruption, including strategic choices, organizational changes, and leadership practices they believe are working.

**Objective 3 (Analysis):** To identify which capabilities and practices correlate with transformation success and how leaders balance innovation with continuity.

**Objective 4 (Contribution):** To develop a framework for leadership during technology disruption in regulated industries.

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Research Design
**Qualitative case study of digital transformation leadership**: Study 2-3 insurance organizations at different stages of digital transformation to understand how leaders navigate disruption.

### 4.2 Data Collection

#### Primary Data: Interviews
- **Sample**: 18-20 interviews including:
  - C-suite (CEO, CTO, COO)
  - Digital and transformation leaders
  - Business unit leaders
  - Technology and innovation leaders
  - HR and culture leaders
  - People who are driving change
  - People navigating the old model alongside new

- **Interview Guide Topics**:
  - Disruption threats perceived and how leadership thinks about them
  - Strategic choices made in response to disruption
  - Organizational changes made (structure, capability, culture)
  - Leadership practices enabling transformation
  - How they balance innovation with continuity
  - Challenges in transformation (resistance, capability gaps, legacy systems)
  - How they develop future-ready leaders
  - Lessons learned

#### Secondary Data: Strategic Documents and Performance Data
- Transformation strategy documents (if accessible)
- Organizational structure and capability maps
- Investment decisions (legacy vs. new technology)
- Talent and retention data
- Customer data (if transformation is working)
- Transformation program status

#### Tertiary Data: Observation and Culture Artifacts
- Observe leadership meetings or transformation reviews if access permits.
- Review communications and culture artifacts about transformation.

### 4.3 Analysis Approach
- **Strategic Mapping**: Document strategic choices and their rationale.
- **Leadership Practice Extraction**: Identify practices leaders believe are enabling transformation.
- **Organizational Pattern Analysis**: How structure, talent, and culture are being redesigned.
- **Comparative Analysis**: Compare approaches across organizations; identify common success factors.

---

## 5. Ethical Considerations

**Low-risk route**:
- Interviews about organizational strategy, leadership practices, and digital transformation.
- Standard consent and confidentiality.

**Key safeguards**:
- Anonymize organization and participants.
- Avoid proprietary transformation strategies or confidential competitive data.
- Secure data storage; GDPR compliance.
