# Topic: Leadership during technology disruption

**Possible Title:** *How insurance leaders navigate competing technology disruptions (AI, blockchain, cloud)*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How leaders prioritize emerging technologies.
- How uncertainty shapes technology investment decisions.
- How leaders balance innovation hype with business value.

## Stronger framing
This topic is about leadership in a fast-moving technology environment. Insurance firms must decide which technologies deserve attention while avoiding distraction, duplication, or premature investment.

## Possible problem statement
The pace of technological change can lead to confusion, fragmented investment, or overcommitment to the wrong solutions. Leaders need a structured way to navigate competing claims and limited resources.

## Possible research questions
- How do leaders evaluate competing technology opportunities?
- What criteria shape technology prioritization?
- How do leaders manage uncertainty and vendor pressure?
- How does technology disruption affect innovation strategy?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Technology evaluation and prioritization.
- Investment governance.
- Vendor influence and internal capability.
- Hype versus value.

## Why this is strong
It is current, relevant, and strongly connected to strategic thinking. It also lets you discuss how leadership creates order in a noisy technology landscape.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
The topic can age quickly if it becomes too tied to a single technology trend. It needs a durable framing around decision-making rather than the tech itself.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How insurance leaders prioritize emerging technologies under uncertainty.
- Leadership strategies for managing technology disruption in insurance.
- Balancing AI, cloud, and other technology investments in regulated firms.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To explore how insurance leaders navigate technology disruption and prioritize investments that create value.

## Likely contribution
A leadership perspective on technology prioritization and strategic discipline.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with technology, strategy, and business leaders.
- Case study of technology prioritization or investment governance.
- Document analysis of roadmaps, business cases, and architecture decisions.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: emerging technology, prioritization, AI, cloud, blockchain, investment governance.
