# Great Dissertations — Summary Table

## Scope

This file summarises the ten example MBA/MA dissertation summaries stored in this folder. Each row links to the full summary file. Use the tables to compare topics, research designs, and contexts, and to choose the best model for your own study.

## Dissertations at a glance

| # | Author (Year) | Title | Topic area | Best for |
|---|---------------|-------|------------|----------|
| 1 | [Arundale (2012)](Arundale%202012_summary.md) | Value creation in non-profit member organisations | Value proposition, member loyalty, brand, social media | Investigating stakeholder value and brand loyalty in a membership/non-profit setting |
| 2 | [Binova (2013)](Binova%202013_summary.md) | Innovation practices in Cambodian microfinance institutions | Innovation types, processes, motivations, barriers | Sector-level innovation study in a developing economy |
| 3 | [Helal (2012)](Helal%202012_summary.md) | Leadership styles and organisational change | Leadership style, change, satisfaction, intention to stay | Pairing a leadership-style questionnaire with employee perceptions |
| 4 | [Janes (2015)](Janes%202015_summary.md) | Exploring resistance to change | Causes of resistance and management responses | A rigorous qualitative case study with a clear conceptual framework |
| 5 | [Jonckheere (2014)](Jonckheere%202014_summary.md) | Effective humanitarian leadership in Sudan | Leadership competencies in a difficult context | Advanced qualitative work linking strong context to leadership behaviour |

## Methodology and context

| Author (Year) | Research design | Approach | Sample and data | Organisation / sector | Country |
|---------------|-----------------|----------|-----------------|-----------------------|---------|
| [Arundale (2012)](Arundale%202012_summary.md) | Cross-sectional mixed-method case study | Survey-led plus interviews | 126 survey responses; 8 interviews | Non-profit membership body ("The Foundation") | UK (international members) |
| [Binova (2013)](Binova%202013_summary.md) | Multiple embedded case study (10 cases) | Qualitative-dominant mixed | 10 C-level executive interviews with rankings | Licensed microfinance institutions | Cambodia |
| [Helal (2012)](Helal%202012_summary.md) | Cross-sectional case study | Predominantly quantitative | 11 leader self-assessments; 76 employee questionnaires | Third-party payment processor ("PI") | UAE, Bahrain, Egypt |
| [Janes (2015)](Janes%202015_summary.md) | Single qualitative case study | Interpretivist, deductive framework | 12 stakeholder interviews (MAXQDA) plus documents | IT function, sporting-goods company | Germany |
| [Jonckheere (2014)](Jonckheere%202014_summary.md) | Exploratory multi-case study | Inductive qualitative | 10 leaders (two interviews each); 4 donors | International humanitarian NGOs | Sudan |
| [Kallmyr (2017)](Kallmyr%202017_summary.md) | Exploratory cross-sectional case study | Inductive qualitative | 10 middle-manager interviews | Large telecom ("Company T", ~112,000 staff) | Sweden |
| [Mares (2021)](Mares%202021_summary.md) | Exploratory cross-sectional case study | Inductive qualitative plus 2 surveys | 10 interviews (NVivo), 66% of firm | Small financial-services firm ("Company X", 15 staff) | UK (London) |
| [Mitra (2021)](Mitra%202021_summary.md) | Exploratory cross-sectional case study | Qualitative, reflexive thematic, abductive | 12 interviews, 29% of workforce | Personal-care/fragrance distributor ("Company A", 42 staff) | UAE / GCC |
| [Ormerod (2014)](Ormerod%202014_summary.md) | Cross-sectional mono-method survey | Quantitative, deductive/positivist | 1,059 usable survey responses | UK schools (classroom technology) | UK |
| [Squires (2014)](Squires%202014_summary.md) | Sequential mixed-method case study | Exploratory inductive | 10 interviews, then 164 survey responses | Public health service (~4,700 staff, "AnHHS") | Australia (Queensland) |
