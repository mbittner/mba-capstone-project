# Dissertation Proposal: Strategic Innovation in Legacy Organizations

## Title
**How Legacy Insurance Firms Can Balance Operational Stability with Innovation**

---

## 1. Research Background and Context

### 1.1 The Problem in Insurance
Legacy insurers face a strategic paradox: the core business requires disciplined operations, risk management, and full reliability. Yet the future requires innovation, experimentation, and adaptability. Resources devoted to modernizing legacy systems leave less for innovation. Leaders must choose: Do we invest in keeping the lights on or investing in the future?

The challenge: How do mature insurance firms make strategic innovation choices that don't starve core operations? And how do leaders think about resource allocation between stability and transformation?

This dissertation examines how legacy insurance organizations balance these competing imperatives and what strategic choices enable both stability and innovation.

### 1.2 Why This Topic Is Important
1. **Realistic Strategic Challenge**: Every mature insurer faces this tension; understanding it is strategically valuable.
2. **Strategic Thinking Theme**: Core MBA topic; directly applicable to executive decision-making.
3. **High Stakes**: Poor choices can either undermine core business or fail to modernize.
4. **Consulting Relevance**: Consultants advise on these strategic choices constantly.
5. **Insurance-Specific**: The challenge is amplified in insurance due to operational complexity and risk profile.

---

## 2. Research Question and Objectives

### 2.1 Primary Research Question
**How do legacy insurance organizations prioritize and allocate resources between sustaining core operations and investing in strategic innovation, and what strategic governance frameworks most effectively enable both stability and transformation?**

### 2.2 Research Objectives

**Objective 1 (Literature):** To critically review literature on organizational strategy, resource allocation, portfolio management, innovation in mature firms, and dual operating models, with focus on regulated and capital-intensive industries.

**Objective 2 (Evidence):** To explore how senior leaders in legacy insurance firms make strategic innovation decisions, including how they frame the stability-innovation tradeoff and what factors influence prioritization.

**Objective 3 (Analysis):** To assess which strategic frameworks and governance approaches best balance stability and innovation and which investment patterns correlate with business sustainability.

**Objective 4 (Contribution):** To develop a strategic framework for legacy organizations managing simultaneous stability and transformation.

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Research Design
**Strategic case study**: Study one or two legacy insurance organizations' strategic innovation decisions, examining how leaders frame and manage the stability-innovation tradeoff.

This approach allows you to:
- Understand real strategic choices and their consequences.
- Link strategy decisions to outcomes.
- Identify governance approaches that balance competing demands.

### 4.2 Data Collection

#### Primary Data: Interviews
- **Sample**: 15-20 interviews including:
  - C-suite or strategic leaders
  - Portfolio or innovation owners
  - CFO or capital allocation leaders
  - Business unit heads
  - Risk or compliance leads (who constrain strategy)

- **Interview Guide Topics**:
  - How strategic priorities are set
  - How the stability-innovation tradeoff is framed
  - What criteria guide resource allocation
  - How governance structures support strategic choices
  - What tensions arise and how they're resolved
  - Post-decision outcomes and learning

#### Secondary Data: Strategic Documents
- Strategic plans and board materials
- Capital allocation frameworks or policies
- Innovation portfolio/program lists
- Business cases and investment decisions
- Risk frameworks and constraints
- Performance results

#### Tertiary Data: Organizational Charts and Governance
- Structure of strategy, portfolio, and capital allocation functions
- Decision-making and governance processes

### 4.3 Analysis Approach
- **Strategic Framing Analysis**: How do leaders conceptualize the stability-innovation choice?
- **Governance Mapping**: What structures and processes support balanced allocation?
- **Outcome Tracking**: Do certain strategic approaches yield better results?
- **Comparative Analysis**: If two organizations, compare strategic choices and outcomes.

## 5. Ethical Considerations

**Low-risk route**:
- Interviews about business strategy (appropriate research topic).
- No sensitive personal data; business strategy is fair game.
- Standard consent and confidentiality.

**Key safeguards**:
- Anonymize organization and participants.
- Avoid disclosing proprietary strategy or capital allocation details.
- Secure data storage; GDPR compliance.
- Discuss confidentiality boundaries with organizational contact.
