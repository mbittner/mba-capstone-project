# Dissertation Proposal: Diversity and Inclusion as Innovation Drivers

## Title
**Leveraging Diversity and Inclusion to Accelerate Innovation in Insurance Organizations**

---

## 1. Research Background and Context

### 1.1 The Problem in Insurance
Insurance organizations typically underrepresent women, minorities, and diverse backgrounds, particularly in leadership and technology roles. This matters not only for fairness but for innovation: diverse teams think differently, challenge assumptions more, and generate more creative solutions. Yet many insurance organizations treat diversity and inclusion as an HR compliance initiative rather than an innovation strategy.

The question: How do insurance leaders leverage diversity and inclusion (D&I) initiatives to strengthen innovation capability? What practices make diverse teams innovative?

This dissertation examines the relationship between diversity, inclusion, and innovation in insurance and what organizational practices make diverse teams innovative.

### 1.2 Why This Topic Is Important
1. **Research-Backed**: Strong evidence that diversity drives innovation (McKinsey, Boston Consulting Group).
2. **Strategic Business Value**: Leveraging diversity for innovation is a competitive advantage.
3. **Leadership Imperative**: Leaders must create inclusive environments where diverse perspectives contribute to innovation.
4. **Measurable**: Can assess diversity, inclusion climate, and innovation outcomes.
5. **Insurance-Specific**: Insurance lags many industries in D&I; opportunity for competitive advantage through deliberate practice.

---

## 2. Research Question and Objectives

### 2.1 Primary Research Question
**How do insurance leaders create inclusive environments and organizational practices that leverage diversity to strengthen innovation capability, and which D&I approaches most effectively translate diversity into innovation performance?**

### 2.2 Research Objectives

**Objective 1 (Literature):** To critically review literature on diversity and innovation, team composition, inclusion climate, psychological safety, unconscious bias, and innovation team dynamics.

**Objective 2 (Evidence):** To explore how insurance organizations approach diversity and inclusion, and how well they are leveraging it for innovation.

**Objective 3 (Analysis):** To identify which D&I practices and organizational approaches most effectively enable diverse teams to innovate.

**Objective 4 (Contribution):** To develop practical guidance for insurance leaders on linking D&I to innovation strategy.

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Research Design
**Mixed-method case study**: Combine interviews with diversity metrics and innovation outcomes to explore the diversity-innovation link in insurance teams.

### 4.2 Data Collection

#### Primary Data: Interviews
- **Sample**: 16-18 interviews including:
  - HR and D&I leaders
  - Innovation and business leaders
  - Team leaders managing diverse teams
  - Members of high-performing diverse teams
  - People from underrepresented groups (and their experience)
  - People who witness inclusion or exclusion in teams

- **Interview Guide Topics**:
  - How diversity is valued and articulated
  - D&I initiatives and their intended purpose
  - Connection between D&I and innovation
  - How team diversity is managed and leveraged
  - Experience of inclusion/exclusion in innovation contexts
  - Whether diverse perspectives are sought and valued
  - Examples of diversity strengthening innovation
  - Barriers to leveraging diversity (bias, hierarchies, etc.)

#### Secondary Data: Diversity and Innovation Metrics
- Team composition data (diversity demographics)
- Inclusion survey results (sense of belonging, voice)
- Innovation metrics (ideas submitted, pilots, adoption)
- Engagement and retention data by demographic group
- D&I initiative portfolio and resource allocation

#### Tertiary Data: Document and Observation
- Review D&I policies and initiatives.
- Observe innovation forums or team meetings to assess voice and participation by demographic group.

### 4.3 Analysis Approach
- **Diversity-Innovation Correlation**: Link team diversity to innovation outcomes.
- **Inclusion Climate Analysis**: Assess how inclusion affects participation and idea contribution.
- **D&I Practice Mapping**: Document D&I initiatives and intended innovation impact.
- **Thematic Analysis**: Extract mechanisms through which diversity drives innovation.

---

## 5. Ethical Considerations

**Low-risk route**:
- Interviews about team diversity, inclusion, and innovation (standard workplace topics).
- Demographic data aggregated (no individual identification).
- Optional survey using validated inclusion and innovation climate scales.

**Key safeguards**:
- Anonymize participants and organization.
- Protect identity of people from underrepresented groups.
- Avoid questions that expose personal bias or discrimination.
- Secure data storage; GDPR compliance.
