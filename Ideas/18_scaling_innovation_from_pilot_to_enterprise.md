# Topic: Scaling innovation: from pilot to enterprise

**Possible Title:** *Leadership challenges in scaling innovation from pilot programs to enterprise-wide adoption in insurance*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- Why some pilots succeed but never scale.
- What leadership actions help move from experiment to enterprise adoption.
- How governance and culture affect scaling decisions.

## Stronger framing
This topic studies the difficult middle ground between experimentation and full adoption. It is highly relevant in insurance, where many innovation pilots show promise but fail to gain enterprise traction.

## Possible problem statement
Organizations often celebrate pilots without building the governance, capability, and sponsorship needed to scale them. The result is repeated experimentation without meaningful enterprise impact.

## Possible research questions
- What prevents successful pilots from scaling?
- Which leadership actions support enterprise adoption?
- How do governance and operating model choices affect scale?
- What role do culture and capability play in scaling innovation?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Transition from pilot to implementation.
- Governance and sponsorship.
- Operating model readiness.
- Capability and change management.

## Why this is strong
It is one of the most practical innovation topics because scaling is where business value is actually created. It is also highly relevant to insurance modernization efforts.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
To study this well, you need access to a pilot and its follow-through, which may be difficult. The topic can also become too broad if you include every scaling barrier at once.

## Sharper version of the topic
- Leadership challenges in moving innovation from pilot to enterprise adoption.
- Why insurance innovation pilots fail to scale.
- Governance and leadership in scaling innovation across the enterprise.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To understand the leadership, governance, and organizational factors that determine whether innovation scales beyond the pilot stage.

## Likely contribution
A practical explanation of how to convert promising experiments into enterprise value.

## Best methodology options
- Case study of a pilot-to-scale journey.
- Interviews with sponsors, delivery teams, and operational owners.
- Review of implementation, governance, and adoption evidence.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: scaling, pilot, enterprise adoption, governance, implementation, operating model, business value.
