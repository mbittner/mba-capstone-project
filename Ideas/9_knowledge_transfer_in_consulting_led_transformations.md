# Topic: Knowledge transfer in consulting-led transformations

**Possible Title:** *How consulting firms can embed sustainable change through effective knowledge transfer*

## Refined dissertation ideas
- How knowledge transfer affects long-term transformation success.
- Why change stalls when consultants leave too early.
- How internal capability can be built during delivery.

## Stronger framing
This topic treats knowledge transfer as the mechanism that turns project delivery into sustainable change. It is especially strong in consulting where external teams often create the solution but the client must own the outcome.

## Possible problem statement
Many consulting-led transformations create dependence on external experts. If knowledge is not transferred effectively, the client struggles to maintain, adapt, or scale the change.

## Possible research questions
- What makes knowledge transfer effective in transformation programs?
- How do consultants and clients share ownership of capability building?
- Which practices improve retention and use of knowledge?
- How does knowledge transfer affect sustainability of change?

## Possible sub-focus areas
- Formal and informal knowledge transfer.
- Client capability building.
- Documentation versus practice-based learning.
- Transition and handover governance.

## Why this is strong
It addresses a concrete pain point in consulting and gives you a topic with direct professional relevance. It also has a strong link to sustainable change.

## Why this could be hard to achieve
Knowledge transfer is often invisible and hard to measure. The topic needs a well-defined transformation context to avoid becoming too broad or anecdotal.

## Sharper version of the topic
- How consulting teams transfer capability during insurance transformations.
- The role of knowledge transfer in sustainable consulting-led change.
- What prevents knowledge from sticking after a transformation program.

## Suggested dissertation aim
To understand how effective knowledge transfer supports sustainable change in consulting-led insurance transformations.

## Likely contribution
A practical guide to reducing post-project dependency and improving client capability.

## Best methodology options
- Interviews with consultants and client stakeholders.
- Case study of a transformation handover or transition phase.
- Review of training materials, transition plans, and governance records.

## Other relevant information
Useful keywords: knowledge transfer, capability building, handover, sustainability, consulting delivery, client ownership.
